Time-bound evaluation policy — proposed, not active

Seven-day signed, revocable entitlements; named identity; least-privilege module scopes; expiration checked on every protected cloud request. One active cloud session, up to 50 read-only runs, 10 MiB per file, 50 MiB per tenant, with uploaded files scheduled for deletion within 24 hours. Each upload requires authorization and explicit consent. The customer pays no licence fee under an approved free pilot, but Fractal5 will not fund cloud compute without a preapproved maximum spend and automated cutoff.

Windows download links, once authorized, are short-lived and tied to a certified digest; the executable also needs its own signed runtime entitlement. A time-limited download URL cannot revoke an installed program. Truly offline executable functionality cannot promise instant revocation; premium and networked functions need trusted-server checks and short renewable leases. The public offline HTML demonstration does not expire and carries no production rights.

Customer-owned Google Cloud deployment is a third, separately certified path.

Future signed/minimized deployment bundles require independent source/image digests, secure customer-controlled bootstrap, isolated IAM, legal rights, tested revocation, and customer-project acceptance. Google Cloud charges may apply to customer-owned infrastructure. Neither a provider bundle nor a commercial Google Cloud runtime is offered for public download today.
