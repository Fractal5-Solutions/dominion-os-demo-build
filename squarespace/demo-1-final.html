CLAIM DISCIPLINE

Four words. Four different proofs.

These terms are intentionally non-interchangeable across the website, release registry, demonstrations, and sales material.

LIVE

A runtime is currently operating with fresh evidence sufficient for the claim.

DOWNLOADABLE

A real, versioned public artifact exists and can actually be downloaded.

DEPLOYABLE

A defined deployment package or path has earned the required implementation evidence.

DEMONSTRATED

A defined capability or workflow has observable evidence showing what occurred.

EVIDENCE ROOM

Evidence, not theatre.

Public proof remains deliberately narrow. Private source, secrets, credentials, customer data, signing material, private APIs, and production controls stay outside this surface.
