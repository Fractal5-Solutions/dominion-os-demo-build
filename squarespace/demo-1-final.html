Dominion OS is a governed operating environment for coordinating missions through intelligence, decisions, workflows, software, AI, and evidence. Its public demonstration and distribution surfaces remain deliberately separated from private source code, credentials, customer information, signing material, production controls, private APIs, and operational authority.

THE DOMINION OPERATING MODEL

Mission → Situation → Intelligence → Options → Decision → Action → Evidence

Dominion organizes work around the mission rather than around disconnected software modules.

01

Mission

Define the objective, authorities, constraints, and desired outcome.

02

Situation

Understand current conditions, actors, systems, risks, and dependencies.

03

Intelligence

Structure sources, evidence, analysis, uncertainty, and forecasts.

04

Options

Compare scenarios, trade-offs, consequences, and courses of action.

05

Decision

Apply policy, judgment, approvals, and authority.

06

Action

Move approved work into bounded execution and coordinated workflows.

07

Evidence

Preserve inputs, approvals, outputs, state, and receipts.

CLAIM STATES

Four words. Four different proofs.

LIVE

A runtime is currently operating with evidence sufficient for the public claim.

DOWNLOADABLE

A real, versioned artifact exists and can actually be downloaded.

DEPLOYABLE

A defined deployment package or path has earned the required implementation evidence.

DEMONSTRATED

A defined capability or workflow has observable evidence showing what occurred.
